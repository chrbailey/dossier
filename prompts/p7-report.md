# Phase 7: Report Assembly

## Goal
Compile all phase outputs into a unified due diligence report with executive summary and confidence matrix. This is the final deliverable.

## Inputs
Read ALL prior phase outputs:
- `output/{DOMAIN}/01-discovery.md`
- `output/{DOMAIN}/02-market.md`
- `output/{DOMAIN}/03-technical.md`
- `output/{DOMAIN}/04-claims.md`
- `output/{DOMAIN}/05-academic.md`
- `output/{DOMAIN}/06-valuation.md`
- `output/{DOMAIN}/social-signals-x.md` (if exists — Top 100 X accounts)

Also read the templates:
- `templates/prd-template.md` (structure for main report)

## Steps

### 7.1 Synthesis Pass
Read all phase outputs. Identify:
- Cross-references (e.g., market claims that P4 validated/invalidated)
- Contradictions between phases (e.g., claimed team size vs LinkedIn signals)
- Strongest and weakest evidence areas
- Overall narrative arc (what story does the data tell?)

### 7.2 Confidence Matrix
Rate each section on two dimensions:

**Data Quality** (how good is the underlying data?):
- HIGH: Multiple independent sources, verifiable facts
- MEDIUM: Single source or partially corroborated
- LOW: Inference, estimation, or limited data

**Analysis Confidence** (how reliable is the analysis?):
- HIGH: Clear evidence supports conclusion
- MEDIUM: Reasonable inference with some uncertainty
- LOW: Speculative or based on weak signals

### 7.3 Executive Summary
Write a 2-page executive summary covering:
1. **Company Overview** — what they do, for whom, how big (2-3 sentences)
2. **Key Strengths** — top 3 things going for them
3. **Key Risks** — top 3 concerns or red flags
4. **Technical Assessment** — architecture quality, open source health, real vs claimed capability
5. **Market Position** — where they sit competitively, growth trajectory
6. **Valuation Signal** — rough replication cost, business model viability
7. **Recommendation** — overall assessment (investigate further / proceed with caution / strong candidate / pass)

### 7.4 Unified Report
Fill in the PRD template with content from all phases. Every section should:
- Lead with the conclusion
- Follow with supporting evidence
- Note confidence level
- Flag any gaps or recommended follow-up

### 7.5 Methodology Blind Spots (MANDATORY — DO NOT OMIT)

Every report MUST include this section. It is not optional. Copy this verbatim
and fill in the company-specific context:

```markdown
## What This Analysis Cannot See

This analysis is based entirely on publicly available data. It is a screening
tool, not a substitute for traditional due diligence with data room access,
management interviews, and customer calls.

**This methodology cannot access:**

- **Financial internals:** Revenue breakdown by product/segment, gross margins,
  burn rate, runway, cash position, unit economics
- **Customer data:** Actual churn rate, NRR, LTV/CAC, pipeline, contract terms,
  win/loss ratios, customer concentration details
- **Legal exposure:** Pending litigation, regulatory investigations, IP disputes
  in discovery, compliance audit results
- **Cap table:** Dilution, preference stacks, liquidation waterfalls, secondary
  sale prices, investor rights
- **Board dynamics:** Investor pressure, founder-board conflicts, governance
  issues, voting control
- **Internal roadmap:** Actual priorities vs. marketing roadmap, killed projects,
  pivots in progress, technical debt reality

**Recommended follow-up to fill these gaps:**
- [Company-specific recommendations based on findings]
```

### 7.6 Recommended Next Steps
Based on all findings, recommend:
- What additional due diligence is needed (if any)
- Specific questions to ask the company directly
- Areas where a deeper technical review is warranted
- Timeline and cost for recommended next steps
