4. **Financial/revenue signals:** No CFO or finance team presence on X. Revenue growth (82% YoY claimed) cannot be verified through social signals.

5. **Security team presence:** Despite major CVEs (CVSS 9.1 and 10.0), Vercel's internal security researchers have minimal X presence. Vulnerability response quality is visible only through external researchers.

6. **Sales/GTM signals:** Jeanne DeWitt Grosser (COO, ex-Stripe CBO) has limited X presence. Enterprise sales motion signals are largely invisible.

7. **Competitor intelligence depth:** Railway, Render, and Fly.io employees rarely engage directly about Vercel on X. Competitive intelligence is mostly one-directional (Vercel -> competitors, not reverse).
