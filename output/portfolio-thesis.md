## Executive Summary

This portfolio represents a near-complete AI infrastructure stack — from silicon (Cerebras) through identity (Okta), deployment (Vercel), enterprise search (Glean), to customer-facing AI (Sierra). Combined, the 5 companies represent ~$64.2B in valuation against ~$3.4B in aggregate revenue, yielding a blended 19x revenue multiple.

**Thesis:** Enterprise AI adoption is a multi-decade secular trend. Rather than betting on which AI application wins, this portfolio owns the infrastructure layers that all AI applications require. The diversification across moat types (hardware, network effect, community, operational, relationship) provides protection against any single competitive dynamic.

**Risk:** The portfolio's correlated exposure to enterprise AI spending cycles is the dominant systemic risk. An "AI winter" would impair 4 of 5 holdings (Okta partially insulated).

| **Vercel** | 18% | $18M | Real community moat (Next.js). AI re-acceleration story is compelling. But 46.5x demands near-perfect execution; sized smaller due to IPO compression risk. |
| **Sierra AI** | 12% | $12M | Smallest allocation due to highest risk — 67–100x multiple with zero disclosed SaaS metrics. Sized for the trajectory bet, not the fundamentals. |

### Allocation Rationale

**Barbell strategy:** 50% in lower-risk, proven businesses (Okta + Glean) and 50% in higher-risk, higher-reward bets (Cerebras + Vercel + Sierra). The lower-risk half provides stability and cash generation; the higher-risk half provides asymmetric upside.

**Why not equal-weight?** Equal weighting would overexpose the portfolio to Sierra's opacity and Cerebras' customer concentration. Risk-adjusted sizing puts more capital where the evidence is strongest.

## 5. What This Portfolio Does NOT Cover

**Gaps in the AI infrastructure stack:**
- **Foundation models:** No exposure to model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). The portfolio is a bet that the application/infrastructure layer captures more durable value than the model layer.
- **Data infrastructure:** No Snowflake, Databricks, or data pipeline company. Enterprise AI requires data infrastructure that none of these 5 provide.
- **Cybersecurity (beyond identity):** Okta covers identity, but no endpoint, network, or cloud security. The Palo Alto + CyberArk combination is a competitive threat, not a portfolio holding.
- **Vertical AI:** No industry-specific AI company (healthcare, legal, finance). Sierra is closest but is horizontal.

**Known unknowns:**
- NRR for 3 of 5 companies (Glean, Vercel, Sierra)
- Gross margins for 2 of 5 companies (Glean, Sierra)
- Customer concentration for 3 of 5 (Glean, Vercel, Sierra)
- Path to profitability for 4 of 5 (all except Okta)
