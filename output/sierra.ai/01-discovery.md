### Founding Story & Narrative Arc

**Stealth phase (2023):** Bret Taylor (fresh from Salesforce co-CEO, OpenAI board chair) and Clay Bavor (18-year Google veteran who ran Workspace and AR/VR) reconnect. They originally met as Google APMs nearly two decades ago. They raise $110M seed from Sequoia and Benchmark at ~$1B valuation before writing a line of public-facing code. This is one of the largest seed rounds in enterprise SaaS history, anchored entirely on founder reputation.

**Launch (Feb 13, 2024):** Sierra exits stealth with a Fortune exclusive, initial customers already live (WeightWatchers, Sonos, SiriusXM, OluKai). The go-to-market is top-down enterprise sales, not product-led growth. The messaging is deliberate: "conversational AI platform for businesses" -- not chatbot, not copilot.

**Hypergrowth (2024-2025):** $20M ARR by late 2024 -> $100M ARR by Nov 2025 (7 quarters, 5x growth). Series A at $4.5B (Oct 2024, $175M from Greenoaks). Series C at $10B (Sep 2025, $350M from Greenoaks). Receptive AI acquisition (Mar 2025) for voice. CNBC Disruptor 50 (Jun 2025). Sierra Summit -- first customer conference (Nov 2025). SoftBank Vision Fund 2 investment + Japan expansion (Dec 2025).

**Current (Feb 2026):** $150M+ ARR (first $50M quarter). Plans to triple headcount. 300K sq ft SF office lease signed (largest in SF in 2025). Seven global offices. Fortune 20 adoption. Voice has surpassed text as primary channel.

### Architecture (from Engineering Blog)

**"Constellation of Models"** is Sierra's core differentiator:
- 15+ models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, plus proprietary
- Each model selected for task-specific strengths (speed vs accuracy vs cost)
- Automated failover across providers
- Supervisory layers where a second model verifies the first
- Continuous monitoring: latency, error rates, timeouts
- Load-testing infrastructure at millions-of-calls scale

**Agent SDK** uses declarative programming to define agent behavior:
- Composable skills (modular agent capabilities)
- Deterministic API interactions
- CI/CD tooling for agent deployment
- Multi-agent orchestration

## Key Findings

1. **Fastest enterprise SaaS ramp in recent history.** $0 -> $100M ARR in 21 months is historically rare. The trajectory from $20M (late 2024) to $150M+ (Jan 2026) suggests genuine product-market fit with large enterprises, not just hype-cycle revenue.

2. **Founder brand IS the company brand (for now).** Nearly every press mention leads with "Bret Taylor, ex-Salesforce co-CEO, OpenAI board chair." This accelerated initial trust and enterprise sales cycles but creates key-person risk and makes it hard to assess the company's independent brand equity.

3. **Aggressive international expansion signals confidence in unit economics.** Seven offices globally (SF, NYC, Atlanta, London, Paris, Singapore, Tokyo) within two years of launch, plus the 300K sq ft SF lease (largest in SF 2025) and plans to triple headcount, suggest either genuine revenue momentum or aggressive burn. The SoftBank investment + Japan expansion indicates APAC strategy is investor-backed.

4. **Technical moat is architecture, not models.** The "Constellation of Models" approach (15+ models, automated failover, supervisory layers) means Sierra is not dependent on any single LLM provider. This is a genuine architectural advantage -- if one model degrades, the system routes around it. The research org publishing benchmarks (tau-bench) adds credibility.

5. **Revenue claims require scrutiny at due diligence.** The "95% of US shoppers" and "50% of families in healthcare" claims in the Year Two blog are extraordinary. These likely measure downstream reach through enterprise customers (e.g., R1's 40M calls/year) rather than direct Sierra usage, but the methodology is not disclosed. The $150M ARR figure is a Sacra estimate corroborated by Sierra's blog saying ">$150M" and "first $50M quarter."

## Open Questions

### Must-Answer for Due Diligence

1. **Net revenue retention (NRR)?** Outcome-based pricing means revenue per customer fluctuates. What is the NRR? Are customers expanding or just maintaining?

2. **Gross margin profile?** Running 15+ models per conversation is expensive. What are the unit economics on a per-conversation basis? How much goes to LLM inference costs vs Sierra margin?

3. **Customer concentration?** With $150M ARR and likely <100 enterprise customers, what percentage comes from the top 5? Top 10?

4. **Actual vs claimed containment rates?** 70% containment is strong but self-reported. What happens to the other 30%? What is the handoff experience?

5. **Burn rate and path to profitability?** $635M+ raised, 300+ employees, 7 global offices, 300K sq ft lease. What is the monthly burn? When is profitability expected?

6. **Competitive defensibility without Taylor?** If Bret Taylor left (he has board obligations at OpenAI), what happens to the enterprise sales pipeline?

7. **Voice economics?** Voice has reportedly surpassed text as primary channel. Voice inference is more expensive than text. How does this affect margin?

8. **ChatGPT integration dependency?** Publishing agents to ChatGPT is a distribution channel controlled by OpenAI. What are the terms? Can OpenAI change them?
