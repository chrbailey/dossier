## 5. Product & Market

### Primary Product: Glean Work AI Platform
Three product pillars:

1. **Enterprise Search** — AI-powered search across 100+ SaaS connectors (Slack, Google Workspace, Jira, Salesforce, Confluence, GitHub, etc.). Proprietary Knowledge Graph maps relationships between people, content, and activity.

2. **AI Assistant** — Generative AI answers grounded in enterprise context via RAG. Third-generation assistant (Sep 2025). Model-agnostic: supports Claude 3 Sonnet (Bedrock), Gemini 1.5 Pro (Vertex AI), OpenAI GPT-3.5/4.

3. **AI Agents** — Autonomous enterprise agents (launched Feb 2025). Complete end-to-end tasks across tools using 100+ built-in actions. Agent builder with auto mode enables no-code agent creation. On pace for 1B agent actions by end of 2025.

### Additional Products
- **Glean Apps & APIs** — Custom generative AI agent builder. Launched Jun 2024.
- **Enterprise Graph** — Proprietary context layer combining memory, connectors, indexes, personal/enterprise graphs, and governance. Announced Sep 2025.
- **Glean Prompting** — Advanced prompt customization. Launched with Series E (Sep 2024).
- **Remote MCP Server** — Connects enterprise context with LLMs, IDEs, or agent platforms.
- **On-Premises Deployment** — In collaboration with Dell Technologies (May 2025). Runs on Dell AI Factory infrastructure.

### Target Market
- **Primary:** Mid-market and enterprise organizations (500+ employees)
- **Sweet spot:** Fortune 500 / Global 2000
- **Verticals:** 50+ industries including telecom, banking, retail, travel, manufacturing, semiconductors
- **Buyer:** IT/CIO (platform deployment), individual business units (search/assistant use cases)

### Pricing Model
- Per-user, per-month. NOT publicly listed.
- Base: ~$50/user/month. Gen AI add-on: ~$15/user/month.
- Minimum contract: ~$50-60K/year (100 users)
- Typical enterprise: $100K-$500K/year. Fortune 500: $5M+/year.
- Mandatory 10% support fee on ARR.
- Source: [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/glean), [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/glean-ai-pricing), [GoSearch](https://www.gosearch.ai/blog/glean-pricing-explained/)

## 13. Summary Profile

**Glean Technologies, Inc.** is a Palo Alto-based enterprise AI company founded in 2019 by four former Google engineers, led by CEO Arvind Jain (ex-Google Distinguished Engineer, Rubrik co-founder). Originally incorporated as Scio Technologies, the company rebranded to Glean and launched publicly in 2021.

The company has evolved from an AI-powered enterprise search tool into a full "Work AI" platform spanning search, AI assistant, and autonomous AI agents. It connects to 100+ enterprise SaaS applications, builds a proprietary Knowledge Graph from organizational data, and provides model-agnostic generative AI capabilities with a permissions-aware governance layer.

Glean has raised $765M+ across 6 funding rounds from top-tier investors including Sequoia Capital (led Series C), Lightspeed Venture Partners (co-led Series A, participated in every round), Kleiner Perkins (co-led Series A), General Catalyst (led Series B), Altimeter/DST Global (co-led Series E), and Wellington Management (led Series F). Current valuation is $7.2B (Jun 2025). a16z is referenced in portfolio materials but was NOT confirmed as a named round participant — this requires further verification.

Financial momentum is strong: $200M ARR as of Dec 2025, having doubled from $100M in just 9 months. The $1M+ contract segment tripled. Named customers include Databricks, Duolingo, Grammarly, Sony Electronics, Okta, and Plaid among 100+ enterprise accounts spanning 50+ industries.

The strategic positioning (Feb 2026 TechCrunch) is as "the layer beneath the interface" — an enterprise AI context and governance layer that sits between LLMs and enterprise data, allowing companies to be model-agnostic while maintaining security and permissions compliance.

**Key risk factors to investigate:** Dependence on Google Cloud, competitive pressure from Microsoft Copilot (bundled with M365), burn rate at $200M ARR with 1,400 employees, and the transition from search to agentic AI platform.
