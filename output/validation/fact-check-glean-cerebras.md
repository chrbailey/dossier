**Corrections:**
1. **Date:** The deal was announced **December 24, 2025**, not January 2026. The dossier says "Jan 2026" which is incorrect by about one week.
2. **Structure:** This is technically a non-exclusive licensing agreement + asset acquisition, not a traditional acquisition. Groq continues as an "independent company" under new CEO Simon Edwards. Key personnel (founder Jonathan Ross, president Sunny Madra) join NVIDIA.
3. **Value:** $20B, not directly stated in the dossier but consistent with references.

The dossier's characterization of "NVIDIA acquired Groq" is directionally correct but oversimplifies the deal structure. The date is off by ~1 week.

## Material Findings

### Dossier Strengths
1. **Revenue and funding claims are rock-solid for both companies.** Every financial figure checked out with multiple independent sources.
2. **The Glean founding team error was already caught.** Phase 4 correctly identified T.R. Vishwanath as ex-Facebook, not ex-Google. Good internal quality control.
3. **Cerebras technical specifications are precisely accurate.** Every WSE-3 spec verified to the exact number.
4. **Investor details are accurate.** Round leads, participants, and amounts all verified.

### Dossier Weaknesses
1. **SambaNova-Intel "acquisition" is the most significant error.** The dossier presents this as a done deal ("Intel is acquiring SambaNova (~$1.6B)") when it was a non-binding term sheet that has since stalled. As of Feb 2026, SambaNova is raising funding instead. This affects the "last independent NVIDIA alternative" narrative for Cerebras -- the competitive field is not clearing as completely as the dossier implies.
2. **Groq-NVIDIA deal date and structure oversimplified.** The deal was Dec 24, 2025 (not "Jan 2026") and is structured as a non-exclusive licensing agreement, not a traditional acquisition. Groq continues operating independently.
3. **Cerebras FY2024 revenue range ($272-500M) is very wide.** The dossier acknowledges this uncertainty but the headline "$272M" figure is the low-end estimate. The actual number could be significantly higher.

### Net Assessment
The dossiers are **factually solid on core claims** (revenue, funding, technical specs, key partnerships). The errors found are concentrated in competitive landscape characterizations (SambaNova status, Groq deal structure/timing) rather than in the target companies' own metrics. The most material error -- presenting the SambaNova-Intel deal as confirmed when it has stalled -- affects the Cerebras competitive moat assessment but does not change the fundamental investment thesis.
