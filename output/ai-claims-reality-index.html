Cross-Company Analysis

Five Structural Findings

FINDING 01

Application Layer Beats Infrastructure

Glean and Sierra — the "boring" companies with no proprietary models — offer better risk-adjusted returns than Cerebras. Deepest moat ≠ best investment. Cerebras has physical moat but the deepest risks (customer concentration, margin structure, TSMC dependency).

FINDING 02

Revenue Multiples Are Inverted

Sierra at 67-100x has the highest multiple and weakest disclosed fundamentals. Glean at 36x has the lowest multiple despite equal growth (100%), more defensible moats, and higher ratings. The market is pricing founder brand and narrative over business fundamentals.

FINDING 03

Correlated Systemic Risk

All four companies depend on continued enterprise AI spending momentum. An "AI winter" or budget rationalization hits all simultaneously. Cerebras depends on OpenAI expansion. Sierra and Glean depend on enterprise AI agent budgets. Vercel depends on AI dev tool market growth.

FINDING 04

NRR: The Missing Metric

At 36-100x revenue multiples, Net Revenue Retention is the difference between "durable growth flywheel" and "front-loaded logo acquisition that churns." None of these companies disclose it. Every verdict above is provisional until NRR is verified.

FINDING 05

The Gross Margin Inversion

Deepest AI = worst margins. Cerebras (38-41%), Sierra (est. 40-65%), Glean (est. 65-75%), Vercel (~76%). Each incremental dollar of AI revenue carries higher inference cost than traditional SaaS. The question: at what scale do these margins become self-sustaining?

Methodology Innovation

Six Systematic LLM Biases Identified

Through structured self-audit, we identified six reproducible distortions affecting any LLM-generated business analysis. Net effect: LLMs are systematically bullish on hardware/research and bearish on application companies.

01
Hardware Worship

Overvalues frontier hardware, undervalues enterprise software execution. Training corpus disproportionately covers "breakthrough" hardware. Inflated Cerebras by 20-30%, deflated Glean by 10-20%.

02
Publication Proxy

Equates "publishes papers" with "does real AI." Companies that ship without publishing (Glean, Stripe ML, Apple) are systematically underrated. Absence of publications ≠ absence of innovation.

03
Culture Catastrophizing

10:1 token ratio of culture failures vs. "normal for stage." Pattern-matches any Glassdoor decline to Uber/WeWork archetype. A 4.2 rating at 1,400 employees growing 100% is above peer median.

04
FAANG Wins Prior

"Big tech kills startups via bundling" is over-reinforced. Historical counter-evidence: Slack vs. Teams, Zoom vs. Skype, Figma vs. Adobe, Datadog vs. Azure Monitor. Inflated threats by 20-25%.

05
Description-as-Construction

Most insidious: an LLM that can describe a system fluently underestimates building it in production. The gap between "architecturally achievable" and "production-ready at scale" is where companies die.

06
Post-ZIRP Skepticism

Defaults to "overvalued" for any multiple above 20x, despite historical comparables at similar growth rates commanding 30-100x. Most severely affected Glean (36x for 100% growth = below every comparable).
